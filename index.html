Megalith

Words, set in stone.

About

Megalith turns a short phrase into a block-justified text graphic — every line stretched to fill a common width, the size of each line set by how much text it carries, with different display fonts mixed across the lines. Type a few words, randomize the arrangement, pick your colors or a background image, and export a high-resolution image to share or save.

How to use

  • Type a short phrase — press return for an explicit line break
  • Tap Randomize to re-roll the fonts and line breaks
  • Choose a canvas shape: Square, Portrait, Story, or Poster
  • Set the text color and a background color or photo
  • Share the result or save it to your photo library

Privacy

Megalith collects no personal data and does no tracking. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs — everything happens on your device. Photos you choose as backgrounds are processed locally and never uploaded, and the app only writes to your photo library when you save a graphic you've made.
